Questions

Where are my meals, plates and kitchen stored?

Only on your iPhone. 11 Plates has no account and no server: your meals, plates, kitchen, photos and profile stay on your phone.

How is my daily target worked out?

From your goal, sex, age, height, weight and activity, using a standard energy formula. Change any of them in Profile and tap Update my target. It is an estimate: your doctor or a dietitian can set a better one for you.

Where do the recipes come from?

From you. 11 Plates doesn’t come with its own recipes: you create your plates, with your own steps and photos if you like, or add a recipe from a link. Calories are worked out from the ingredients, so treat them as estimates, and always check the ingredients for your allergies.

How do I add a recipe from a website?

Kitchen → + → Add a recipe from a link. Paste the page and tap Read the recipe: the name, ingredients, steps and photo come in for one portion, and you can check every amount before you save. The plate keeps a link to the original recipe and its author. It works on most recipe sites; if a page can’t be read, create the plate yourself.

Snap your meal got my plate wrong

Reading a dish from a photo is a guess. After it reads the photo, you can fix the name, the foods and every amount before you add it. Photos in daylight, from above, read best.

How do meal reminders work?

Turn them on in Profile, then set a time for each meal or switch a meal off. They are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Since nothing is kept anywhere else, that removes all your data.

Privacy and terms

Your data stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use, including the health and food safety notes.
