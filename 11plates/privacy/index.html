Privacy Policy

11 Plates keeps your meals, kitchen and plates on your phone. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Last updated 28 November 2026

Who we are

Contact: support.11things@gmail.com. Write here for any privacy question or request.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything you enter stays on your phone, in the app’s own storage. There is no account and no copy on our servers.

  • Your profile: goal, sex, age, height, weight, activity and daily kcal target, to work out your target and portions.
  • What you eat: meals, times, amounts and past days, for Today, the calendar and your totals.
  • Your kitchen: products, amounts and photos you take of them, for stock, the shopping list and plate ideas.
  • Your plates: your own plates, My Eleven and photos of your dishes, for your recipe book.
  • Settings: rules (for example no dairy), reminder times, tips on or off.

Some of this is health information (weight, diet, allergies or rules you add). It never leaves your phone unless a feature below sends a part of it, and only when you use that feature.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your phone

Only these features talk to outside services, and only what each one needs is sent. None of them gets your profile, weight or meal history.

  • Snap your meal (reading a dish from a photo): the photo, made smaller, is sent to Pollinations (pollinations.ai) when you take or choose a photo to read.
  • Generate a plate photo: the plate’s name and ingredients are sent to Pollinations when you tap Generate.
  • Cooking mode pictures: the text of each step is sent to Pollinations when a plate has no step pictures of its own.
  • Food search and barcode scan: the words you search or the barcode are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org).
  • App updates: the app version, device type and network address are seen by Expo (expo.dev) when the app checks for an update.

These services see your network (IP) address, like any website you visit. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive. We don’t send them anything that names you.

Meal reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. No server sends them and nothing about your meals leaves the phone.

Permissions

The app asks only when you use the feature, and works without them. You can change them any time in iPhone Settings → 11 Plates.

  • Camera: to photograph a meal or a product, or scan a barcode.
  • Photos: to choose a photo of a meal or product from your library.
  • Notifications: for meal reminders, only if you turn them on in Profile.

Your rights and the legal part

  • Legal basis (EU and UK GDPR): features run because you ask for them. Health details you type stay on your phone and are used only for you, with your consent when you enter them; you can delete them at any time.
  • Your rights: you can access, correct, delete or take your data with you. Since it lives on your phone, you can do most of this in the app or by deleting it. For anything else, write to us; we answer within 30 days.
  • Where it goes: the services above may process requests outside your country, including the United States, only for the feature you start.
  • How long: on your phone until you delete it. We keep no copy.
  • Children: 11 Plates is not meant for anyone under 16.
  • California and other US states: we don’t sell or share personal information for advertising, and we don’t track you across apps.
  • Ukraine: your data is handled in line with the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”.
  • Complaints: you can complain to your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL; in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights).
  • Changes: if this policy changes, the date changes and the app tells you about important changes.
