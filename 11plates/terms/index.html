Health and food safety

  • Not medical advice: recipes, calorie targets and tips are general information. They don’t replace a doctor, dietitian or other health professional.
  • Talk to a professional first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease or another health condition, take medication affected by food, or follow a diet set by a doctor.
  • Eating disorders: if you have or had an eating disorder, counting calories can be harmful. Please use the app only with your care team’s guidance.
  • Allergies and intolerances: always read every ingredient, including on the packs you buy. The app’s allergen hints and your rules can miss things.
  • Cook safely: poultry to 74 °C inside, minced meat to 71 °C, whole cuts of meat to 63 °C with a 3-minute rest, fish until opaque. Keep food cold, wash hands and surfaces, and cool leftovers quickly.
  • Numbers are estimates: calories and nutrients come from average food values and the amounts you enter. Real foods and portions vary.
