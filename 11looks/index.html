Questions

Where are my clothes and looks stored?

Only on your iPhone. 11 Looks has no account and no server: your photos, clothes, looks and plans stay on your phone.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Open Profile, then Backup, then Save a backup. Keep the file in iCloud Drive or Files, or AirDrop it. On the new iPhone, install 11 Looks, open Profile, Backup, Restore from a backup and pick the file.

I bought 11 Looks Unlimited. How do I get it back?

Open Profile, tap the plan card, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. With Family Sharing on, everyone in your family group can restore it too.

What does the free plan include?

Up to 50 clothes and 20 looks, with every feature: My Eleven, rails, your palette, the style advisor and planning. 11 Looks Unlimited removes the limits with one payment. There is no subscription.

The cut-out of my clothing isn't clean

Photograph the clothing flat on a plain background that contrasts with it, in daylight. Tap Fix cut-out to paint the edges by hand, or lift the clothing in Photos (touch and hold it, then Copy) and tap Use iPhone cut-out.

Why don't I see the weather?

Add your city in Profile, or allow location while using the app. The forecast covers the next ten days.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Since nothing is kept anywhere else, that removes all your data. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.
