Privacy policy

Your wardrobe stays on your iPhone. We collect nothing.

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign in.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your photos, clothes, looks, plans and quiz answers are stored only on your device.
  • We never sell or share data, because we don't have any.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to 11 Looks: photos and cut-outs of your clothes, their details, your looks, rails, plans, My Eleven, your style quiz answers, your name, city and profile photo. This data lives in the app's storage on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

If you save a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to you through the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop) is your choice.

Permissions

Photos and camera: only to add the clothing, profile photo or inspiration photo you pick. The app reads only what you choose.

Location (optional, while using the app): only when you haven't added a city, to show the weather beside the days you plan. Your approximate position is used for the forecast and not stored.

Services the app talks to

Apple Weather (WeatherKit): to get the forecast, the app sends Apple the coordinates of your city or rough location. Apple doesn't link this to you. See Apple's weather data sources.

Apple's geocoder: turns the city you type into coordinates for the forecast.

Store pages you link: when you paste a store link for your wishlist, the app opens that page to read its picture, name and price, the same way a browser would.

Category guess: the first time you add a clothing, the app downloads a small image recognition model from Google's servers. Your photos are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Purchases: 11 Looks Unlimited is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, it opens Mail with your note. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to improve 11 Looks and to reply to you.

Children

11 Looks isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: support.11things@gmail.com.
