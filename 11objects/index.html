Questions

Where are my objects, vignettes and rooms stored?

Only on your iPhone. 11 Objects has no account and no server: your photos, objects, vignettes and room photos stay on your phone.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Open Profile, then Backup, then Save a backup. Keep the file in iCloud Drive or Files, or AirDrop it. On the new iPhone, install 11 Objects, open Profile, Backup, Restore from a backup and pick the file.

I bought 11 Objects Unlimited. How do I get it back?

Open Profile, tap the plan card, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. With Family Sharing on, everyone in your family group can restore it too.

What does the free plan include?

Up to 50 objects and 20 vignettes, with every feature: My Eleven, rooms, room photos, your palette and the style advisor. 11 Objects Unlimited removes the limits with one payment. There is no subscription.

The cut-out of my object isn't clean

Photograph the object on a plain background that contrasts with it, in daylight. You can also lift it in Photos (touch and hold the object, then Copy) and tap Use iPhone cut-out when you add it.

How do I put objects behind my furniture on a room photo?

Open the room photo, choose Separate objects, tap the sofa, table or shelf in the photo and press Done. Then select one of your objects and move it backward to place it behind.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Since nothing is kept anywhere else, that removes all your data. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.
