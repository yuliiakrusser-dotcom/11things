Privacy policy

Your home stays on your iPhone. We collect nothing.

Last updated 27 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign in.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your photos, objects, vignettes, room photos and quiz answers are stored only on your device.
  • We never sell or share data, because we don't have any.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to 11 Objects: photos and cut-outs of your objects, their details, your vignettes, rooms and room photos, My Eleven, your style quiz answers, your name, city and profile photo. This data lives in the app's storage on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

Cut-outs and category suggestions are made on your iPhone with Apple's built-in image tools. Your photos are never uploaded.

If you save a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to you through the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop) is your choice.

Permissions

Photos and camera: only to add the objects, room photos, profile photo or inspiration photo you pick. The app reads only what you choose.

Saving to Photos: only when you choose to save a card you share.

11 Objects doesn't ask for your location, contacts or microphone.

Services the app talks to

Store pages you link: when you paste a store link for your wishlist, the app opens that page to read its picture, name and price, the same way a browser would.

Purchases: 11 Objects Unlimited is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, it opens Mail with your note. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to improve 11 Objects and to reply to you.

Children

11 Objects isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: support.11things@gmail.com.
